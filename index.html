The Core Platform

The ThinkOS - On Demand

An end-to-end digital platform connecting wheelie bins, collection crews, commercial operators and councils into a single intelligent network.

Service Flow — How a Collection Happens
1

Request Triggered

Operator or council submits a collection request via the ThinkOS portal, mobile app, or API. Fill-level sensors can also trigger requests automatically.

2

Route Optimised

ThinkOS's AI engine slots the job into the nearest available crew's route, minimising empty kilometres and fuel consumption across the fleet.

3

Driver Dispatched

Driver receives turn-by-turn instructions, bin location, and access notes on the ThinkOS Driver App. RFID confirms the correct bin is lifted.

4

Lift Recorded & Reported

Collection is automatically logged with GPS timestamp, weight data, and bin ID. Operator and council portals update in real time.

5

Invoice & Compliance

Usage-based invoicing issued automatically. Waste audit reports and council compliance documentation generated with zero manual effort.
